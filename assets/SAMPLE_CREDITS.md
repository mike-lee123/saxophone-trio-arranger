# Alto Sax recorded samples

University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios / Lawrence Fritts.
Alto saxophone performed by Michael Giles, November 1997.

Source: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISaltosaxophone.html
Permission statement: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html

The university states the recordings may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions. No additional licence is asserted here. This bank is derived from the six mf Alto Sax chromatic recordings (NoVib and Vib), trimmed into 64 notes, calibrated in pitch and balanced in level. Raw-source checksums are retained in iowa_alto_manifest.json.

This is recorded-note sampling, not a continuous performance recorded for this song. Simulated legato, crossfaded sustained loops and dynamics are explicitly identified in the app and audio reports.
